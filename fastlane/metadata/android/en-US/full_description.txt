Yumi is an open-source Android VPN client built on the Xray core in Material 3 Expressive. It monitors your live tunnel, automatically moves off stalled servers, and stays simple enough to set up once for non-technical users.

Note: Yumi connects only to servers from your own subscriptions or configs and does not provide VPN servers or internet access by itself.

Self-healing tunnel:
- Live traffic health checks every 20 seconds move the tunnel to a working server without dropping open connections or resetting session counters.
- Dedicated cellular server list with automatic switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
- Optional on-device allow-list geo-zones that remember mobile drop areas locally and pre-switch to your cellular server list.
- Fallback DNS resolver (1.1.1.1) if your configured resolver stops answering, with separate notification switches for every automatic repair.

Protocols and subscriptions:
- VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria2, WireGuard, SOCKS, and HTTP.
- Transports: TCP/RAW, WebSocket, gRPC, HTTPUpgrade, XHTTP (splithttp), mKCP, and QUIC with TLS, post-quantum VLESS encryption, REALITY, ECH, Vision, uTLS browser fingerprints, and live packet fragmentation.
- Imports link lists (plain text or base64), Clash YAML, Xray/v2ray JSON, SIP008, QR codes, and deep links (including Happ-compatible provider routing and rules).

Simple and advanced modes:
- Breeze mode (default): plain-language controls with smart defaults, Recommended hints, country/protocol filters, and automatic routing database updates.
- Under the hood mode: full routing rules (Via VPN, Direct, Block), per-app split tunneling, custom DNS resolvers (DoH, TCP, UDP, sdns://), real through-tunnel latency and speed tests, live Connections log, and core journal.
- Quick settings tile, 1x1 home widget, Always-on VPN support, dark/AMOLED themes, and Material You wallpaper palettes.

Android TV remote:
- Pair one or more Yumi TV devices over your local network by QR code and manage TV connections, servers, subscriptions, and DPI settings straight from your phone.

Privacy:
- No telemetry, no analytics, no ads, and no proprietary Google SDKs.
- Subscriptions, keys, and optional geo-zones stay strictly on-device with Android cloud backup disabled.
- Device identifier (x-hwid) sent to subscription panels is random and generated once on install.
- Optional built-in update checker queries GitHub Releases and hands the APK to Android's system installer only when you confirm.
